# Velvet Charms Art & Gifts — prelaunch legal gate

This file is an internal launch checklist. It is not customer-facing copy and must not be treated as legal advice.

## Seller information required before production launch

Do not publish a final Terms / Trader Information page until the real seller details are supplied and verified. Required fields include the trader's legal name, geographic address, direct contact email, trade-register/company registration details where applicable, and VAT details where applicable. These details must be available to the customer before the purchase is concluded.

## Returns and cancellations

- Standard distance-sale goods generally carry the EU 14-day withdrawal right.
- Goods made to the customer's specifications or clearly personalised can fall within the personalised-goods exception in Article 16(c) of Directive 2011/83/EU.
- The personalised-goods exception must be interpreted narrowly and does not remove statutory remedies for faulty, damaged, misdescribed or non-conforming goods.
- The final policy must clearly distinguish ordinary catalogue goods, made-to-order but non-personalised goods, clearly personalised commissions and faulty/non-conforming goods.
- Return-cost responsibility must be disclosed in advance where the ordinary withdrawal right applies.
- If an ordinary withdrawal right applies, the customer must receive the legally required pre-contract information and withdrawal instructions before purchase.

## Shipping

Current site behavior intentionally treats the displayed checkout amount as the product total only. The cart must state clearly that shipping is additional, calculated from destination + parcel size + weight, and that no shipping charge is taken without the customer's approval. Large or unusually fragile pieces may require an individual carrier/packaging quote. Do not display a free-shipping threshold unless a real threshold is later adopted.

## Reference photographs / personal data

- Reference photos are private and are used only to assess/create the requested commission.
- Public portfolio/social-media/marketing use requires separate permission; purchase itself is not marketing consent.
- GDPR principles require purpose limitation, data minimisation, storage limitation and appropriate confidentiality/security measures.
- Custom-request private links expire; the final Privacy Policy must describe the actual operational retention/deletion process accurately rather than promising a period that operations do not follow.
- Final Privacy Policy needs the real controller identity/contact details, legal basis/purposes, retention logic, data-subject rights and the actual processors in use (for example Vercel, Formspree and PayPal as applicable).

## Custom commissions

For a completely new commission, confirm feasibility, price, material, scope, production slot and any agreed reference requirements before the customer is committed to the custom work. Avoid promises of unlimited revisions or guaranteed exact replication of a photograph unless that is genuinely offered.

## General Product Safety Regulation (EU) 2023/988 — hard launch gate for applicable non-food goods

The GPSR has applied since 13 December 2024. Before an applicable product is offered to EU consumers:

- identify the manufacturer / responsible economic operator and keep the required traceability information;
- carry out the applicable product-safety/risk analysis and maintain relevant technical documentation;
- ensure the product is safe for its intended and reasonably foreseeable use;
- identify the product clearly with the information required for traceability;
- determine and provide any warnings or safety information required for the product;
- for online/distance offers, make the manufacturer contact details, product identification and applicable safety/warning information clearly and visibly accessible as required by Article 19 GPSR;
- have a process for complaints, accidents, corrective action, withdrawal/recall and required authority notifications.

Do not invent manufacturer addresses, product identifiers, warnings, age suitability or conformity marks in website copy. Populate these fields only after the physical product and its documentation are final.

## Category-specific product-safety review

Before sale, confirm whether additional EU/national duties apply to each category, particularly:

- candles and candle accessories (fire/burn warnings, stability, vessel suitability and any applicable standards);
- electrical lamps/components (electrical safety, EMC/RoHS/WEEE/CE obligations where applicable);
- jewellery and metal findings (material restrictions such as nickel/restricted substances where applicable);
- textile products (fibre-composition and other applicable labelling);
- products resembling toys or intended for children (do not market as toys/children's products unless the applicable toy-safety route has been assessed);
- resin/clay pieces, decorative objects and items with small/detachable parts (foreseeable-use and choking/injury risks as applicable).

## Official reference points used for this checklist

- Regulation (EU) 2023/988 on general product safety, especially Article 19 for distance-sale offers.
- EU Consumer Rights Directive 2011/83/EU, especially Articles 9 and 16, and current Your Europe B2C distance-selling guidance.
- GDPR principles under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 for customer/reference photographs and other personal data.
- European Commission Safety Gate / product-safety guidance for business obligations.

## Launch decision

Technical readiness is not the same as legal readiness. Art & Gifts can move to production only after the real seller-information fields and applicable product/category safety documentation are confirmed. The website must then display the required product-specific safety/traceability information before the corresponding products are offered for sale.
