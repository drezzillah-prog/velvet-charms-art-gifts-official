# Velvet Charms Art & Gifts — prelaunch legal gate

This file is an internal launch checklist. It is not customer-facing copy and must not be treated as legal advice.

## Seller information required before production launch

Do not publish a final Terms / Trader Information page until the real seller details are supplied and verified. Required fields include the trader's legal name, geographic address, direct contact email, trade-register/company registration details where applicable, and VAT details where applicable. These details must be available to the customer before the purchase is concluded.

## Returns and cancellations

- Standard distance-sale goods generally carry the EU 14-day withdrawal right.
- Goods made to the customer's specifications or clearly personalised can fall within the personalised-goods exception.
- The exception does not remove statutory remedies for faulty, damaged, misdescribed or non-conforming goods.
- The final policy must clearly distinguish ordinary catalogue goods, made-to-order but non-personalised goods, clearly personalised commissions and faulty/non-conforming goods.
- Return-cost responsibility must be disclosed in advance where the ordinary withdrawal right applies.

## Shipping

Current site behavior intentionally treats the displayed checkout amount as the product total only. The cart must state clearly that shipping is additional, calculated from destination + parcel size + weight, and that no shipping charge is taken without the customer's approval. Large or unusually fragile pieces may require an individual carrier/packaging quote.

## Reference photographs / personal data

- Reference photos are private and are used only to assess/create the requested commission.
- Public portfolio/social-media/marketing use requires separate permission; purchase itself is not marketing consent.
- Current private links expire after 30 days. The final Privacy Policy must describe the actual operational retention/deletion process accurately.
- Final Privacy Policy needs the real controller identity/contact details and the actual processors in use (for example Vercel, Formspree and PayPal as applicable).

## Custom commissions

For a completely new commission, confirm feasibility, price, material, scope, production slot and any agreed reference requirements before the customer is committed to the custom work. Avoid promises of unlimited revisions or guaranteed exact replication of a photograph unless that is genuinely offered.

## Product-safety notes

Before sale, confirm any category-specific safety/label duties that apply to candles, electrical lamp components, jewellery findings, children's/toy-like items, textile items and other products. Do not describe a decorative item as a toy or a product for children unless it has been assessed for that intended use.

## Official reference points used for this checklist

- EU Consumer Rights Directive 2011/83/EU and Your Europe guidance on distance selling, withdrawal and personalised goods.
- EU rules on legal guarantees/non-conforming goods.
- GDPR principles for handling customer-supplied personal/reference photographs.

## Launch decision

Technical readiness is not the same as legal readiness. Art & Gifts can move to production only after the seller-information fields and applicable category-specific product-safety checks are confirmed with real information.
