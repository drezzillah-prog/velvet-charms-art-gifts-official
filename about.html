About Velvet Charms

Velvet Charms is a handmade brand focused on thoughtful creations with character. Art & Gifts brings together painting, sculpture, resin, clay, leather, accessories and keepsakes created with patience and attention to detail.

Many pieces are made in small batches, while others begin with your own photograph, person, pet, place, palette or idea. Custom commissions are part of the heart of the studio rather than an afterthought.

Orders & Payments

Orders are placed directly through our website. Payments are processed securely via PayPal, allowing you to use your PayPal balance or, where available, an eligible debit or credit card.

Payment reserves your place in our production schedule. Because every piece is handmade, your production start and estimated dispatch window are confirmed according to the current queue and the complexity of your order.

Contact

For commissions, questions or special requests, please use the Contact page. We usually respond within 1–2 business days.
